## Project Overview
This project involves a comprehensive analysis of historical video game sales data for **GameCo**. The objective was to analyze sales trends, regional preferences, and genre performance to provide data-driven recommendations for future marketing campaigns and product focus.

## Key Findings
Based on the historical analysis of sales data:

* **Sales Trends:** Video game sales increased gradually from 1980, reaching a significant peak between **2008 and 2010**, followed by a decline through 2020.
* **Genre Performance:** The market is dominated by four main genres: **Action, Sports, Shooter, and Role-Playing**. Action games peaked specifically between 2009–2013.
* **Regional Preferences:**
    * **North America & Europe:** Consumers show a strong preference for **Shooter** and **Action** games.
    * **Japan:** **Role-Playing** games are highly successful, whereas Shooter and Racing games have low sales.
    * **Europe:** Shows a higher preference for **Racing** games compared to other regions.
* **Event Impact:** Sports game sales often spike during major real-world events, such as the FIFA World Cup (visible peaks in 1998, 2002, 2006, and 2010).

## Recommendations
To address the current market challenges ("reset stage"), the following strategies are recommended:

1.  **Localized Marketing:**
    * Target **Shooter** games heavily in **North America**.
    * Focus **Role-Playing** game promotions in **Japan**.
    * Highlight **Racing** titles in **Europe**.
2.  **Event Alignment:** Schedule advertising campaigns for Sports titles to coincide with major global sporting events to maximize visibility.
3.  **Market Maintenance:** Continue to prioritize North America for top-tier titles, as it holds the dominant market share for the top 10 selling games.
